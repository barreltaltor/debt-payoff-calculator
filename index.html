Questions

Is the free version really free?
Yes. No email, no trial, no expiry.
Excel or Google Sheets?
The free file is an Excel .xlsx.
How is interest calculated?
Monthly: balance × APR ÷ 12, then your payment. Each debt gets its minimum; whatever is left of your budget goes to the target debt (smallest balance for Snowball, highest APR for Avalanche). When a debt is paid off, its payment rolls to the next one.
What if my budget is too small?
If the plan runs past 10 years, Start says so and shows how much to raise your monthly budget.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. Designed with AI assistance; all formulas tested in Excel.
